EXERCISE 1 – Choose the correct answer

Câu 1: What is "father" in Vietnamese?
A. Mẹ
B. Bố/Ba
C. Anh
D. Chị
Đáp án: B

Câu 2: My mother's sister is my ___.
A. uncle
B. aunt
C. cousin
D. grandmother
Đáp án: B

Câu 3: "Sibling" means:
A. Chỉ anh/chị
B. Chỉ em
C. Anh chị em ruột
D. Bạn bè
Đáp án: C

Câu 4: Which word is NOT a family member?
A. Brother
B. Teacher
C. Cousin
D. Nephew
Đáp án: B

Câu 5: My father's brother is my ___.
A. aunt
B. uncle
C. cousin
D. grandfather
Đáp án: B

EXERCISE 2 – Fill in the blank

Câu 6: She is my mother's mother. She is my ___.
Đáp án: grandmother

Câu 7: He is my father's father. He is my ___.
Đáp án: grandfather

Câu 8: My sister and I are my parents' ___.
Đáp án: children

EXERCISE 3 – True or False

Câu 9: "Son" means "con gái". (Đúng/Sai)
Đáp án: Sai

Câu 10: "Daughter" means "con gái". (Đúng/Sai)
Đáp án: Đúng
